## Placement that survives cropping and reposting

A small mark in the bottom-right corner will be cropped out by the first person who reposts the image to a square format. Watermarks that survive sit near the centre or repeat diagonally across the frame, and they overlap the subject just enough that removal would ruin the picture.

For client previews, a diagonal repeating mark at 15–25% opacity is the usual compromise: the client can judge the photo, but a screenshot is not usable as a final delivery.

## Text vs logo, and opacities that work

A studio name in a clean font is faster and looks less cluttered than a full logo, especially on a busy background. Export your logo as a transparent PNG at two sizes, one for portrait crops and one for landscape.

Opacity below 10% disappears after a messaging app recompresses the image. Above 40% it starts to feel like an advertisement. Most professional marks land between 18% and 30%, with a subtle shadow or a thin stroke so they stay readable on both bright and dark areas.

## Batch watermarking a wedding or catalogue set

Set the position and opacity once, select the whole folder and let the batch run. Keep the original files untouched in a separate directory and write watermarked copies to a new one. Name them clearly, for example prefixing with preview, so clients never receive a marked file by mistake.

Because the work is local, a large set of full-resolution photos does not depend on your upload speed. A slow connection no longer means an hour of waiting.

## FAQs (FAQPage schema ready)

### Does a watermark legally protect my photo?

It discourages casual reuse. Legal protection comes from copyright, which you already hold as the creator, plus evidence such as RAW files and the original upload date. A watermark makes deliberate commercial reuse obvious, which helps in a takedown claim.

### Can I watermark many photos with the same settings?

Yes. Batch mode applies the same text or logo, position, size, rotation and opacity to every selected file.

### Are full-resolution photos uploaded to your server?

No. Watermarking is done in your browser. Original files stay on your device.

### How do I remove a watermark from my own old file?

Use the original unmarked file if you still have it. Removing a mark from a flattened JPEG damages the pixels underneath, so keep a clean master copy of every delivery.
