## Why browser-based beats a server upload

Traditionally a compressor takes your file, sends it to a server, processes it and sends it back. That means your ID scan or bank statement sits in someone else's temporary storage, however brief the promise.

Here the encoder runs on your own device. It is faster on a weak connection, works offline after the first load, and gives a straight answer to the question every privacy-conscious user asks: where did my file go? Nowhere.

## FAQs (FAQPage schema ready)

### Are all the tools really free?

Yes. There is no paid tier, no upload quota and no watermark on the output. The site is funded by advertising, which is why you will see a few clearly marked ad slots.

### Do I need to create an account?

No account is needed for any tool. Nothing is stored, so there is nothing to sign into.

### Do the tools work offline?

Once the page has loaded, most processing tools keep working if the connection drops, because the work happens locally.

### Which tools work on a phone?

All of them. The interface is built mobile-first, since most visitors arrive from a phone browser.
