## Start with the shape the platform forces

A square crop for Instagram and WhatsApp profiles, 4:5 for feed portraits, 9:16 for Stories and Reels, 16:9 for YouTube and website heroes. Picking the ratio first stops the platform from cutting off the top of someone's head later.

For a circular profile photo, crop a square around the face and let the tool apply a circular mask. Keep the eyes around one third from the top: that single adjustment is what makes a profile shot look professional.

## The rule of thirds, applied

Turn on the guide grid and place the eyes, the horizon or the product's label on a third line rather than dead centre. Centred composition works for ID photos and logos; off-centre works for nearly everything else.

Zoom into the crop before exporting. Details that look fine at fit-to-window scale often show sharpening halos or sensor noise at 100%, and an over-sharpened crop looks worse than a slightly soft one.

## Crop before you resize or compress

Order matters: crop first to remove pixels you do not need, then resize to the required dimensions, then compress to hit a byte limit. Doing it in the other order means compressing data you were about to throw away, and often the last step over-shrinks an already small file.

Keep the original. A crop is destructive once saved, so work on a duplicate and store the full frame for future use.

## FAQs (FAQPage schema ready)

### Can I crop to an exact size in pixels?

Yes. Enter fixed width and height in the ratio fields, then drag the crop box. The export matches those dimensions exactly.

### How do I make a circular profile picture?

Crop a square around the face and enable the circle mask. Export as PNG so the area outside the circle keeps transparency.

### Does cropping reduce image quality?

No. Cropping only removes pixels outside the frame and leaves the remaining pixels untouched. Quality is lost later if you resize up or compress heavily.

### Can I crop multiple images to the same frame?

Yes, batch crop keeps one ratio and applies it to every selected file, which is useful for product catalogues.
