## Find out what is heavy first

Open the PDF and check whether it is a typed document or a scan. A typed document of five pages under 300KB is already efficient and will barely compress. A scan of five pages at 8MB is mostly image data, and that is where real savings exist.

## The three settings that matter

Image resolution down to 150 DPI, JPEG quality around 75%, and grayscale for text-only scans. Those three together typically take a 5MB scan to well under 200KB. Dropping to 100KB usually requires 120–150 DPI plus careful quality control, and photos inside the document will look soft.

## Verify before you upload

Zoom to 100% and read the smallest text: an account number, a date of birth, a seal. If it is fuzzy, you have gone too far. Portals reject unreadable documents during manual verification even when the size limit is met, and you lose a day re-submitting.

## What not to do

Do not print to PDF at a screenshot resolution and call it compression, and do not convert pages to low-quality JPEGs inside Acrobat's export menu without checking the text layer. Both destroy selectable text, which some portals use to validate document numbers automatically.

## When the limit is impossible

Some portals demand 100KB for a six-page scanned certificate. In that case split the document: upload the identity page and the marks page as separate files if the form allows multiple uploads, or reduce the scan to the essential page. Splitting is an accepted workaround and beats a rejected blurry file.

## FAQs

**Does compressing a PDF remove the text layer?**

Not if the compression only re-encodes images. Always open the result and try selecting text to confirm.

**Can I compress a PDF on my phone?**

Yes, and it is often faster because the browser-based compressor works locally. Avoid emailing the file to yourself as a first step.

**Why is my PDF still large after compression?**

Embedded fonts, form fields and vector art add weight that image optimisation cannot remove. Very large page dimensions and hundreds of bookmarks are other common causes.
