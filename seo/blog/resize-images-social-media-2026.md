## Editorial angle

## The sizes worth memorising

Instagram feed portrait 1080 x 1350, square 1080 x 1080, Stories and Reels 1080 x 1920. YouTube thumbnail 1280 x 720 under 2MB. LinkedIn banner 1584 x 396, profile 400 x 400. X post image 1600 x 900. WhatsApp status 1080 x 1920, and WhatsApp will still shrink a large photo before sending unless you choose document mode.

## Upload at the target size, not above it

Sending a 6000px photo to Instagram does not make it sharper; the platform re-encodes it anyway. Exporting at the recommended size gives you control over sharpening instead of leaving it to their pipeline.

## Why your upload looks softer after posting

Every platform recompresses. Fine text and low-contrast gradients suffer first. Export at quality 85–90, sharpen very lightly, and avoid translucent overlays and heavy noise reduction, which turn into visible blocking.

## Text-safe zones

Keep captions and logos away from the outer 10% of the frame. Stories and Reels overlay interface elements on the bottom quarter, and a square-crop preview will cut the sides of a portrait image.

## FAQs

**Should I upload PNG or JPG to social media?**

JPG for photographs and PNG for graphics with text or transparency. Platforms re-encode either way, but PNG handles flat colour and sharp type better.

**Why does WhatsApp shrink my photos so much?**

Normal photo sending applies aggressive compression for speed. Sending as a document preserves the original quality, including metadata.

**Do these sizes change often?**

Yes, so treat this page as a starting point and check the platform's current requirements before a paid campaign.
