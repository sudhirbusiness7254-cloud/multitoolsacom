# Advertiser Disclosure

**Live URL:** https://multitoolsa.com/advertiser-disclosure

## What reviewers look for

How the site is funded, that ads are clearly labelled, and that advertisers do not influence tool output.

## Draft notes

One honest paragraph beats a page of boilerplate. Explain that hosting costs are covered by display advertising and that no tool result is ever affected by an advertiser.

## Minimum content depth

300+ words of specific, human-written text. Name the operator, explain the process, and include a working contact route.
