# Privacy Policy

**Live URL:** https://multitoolsa.com/privacy

## What reviewers look for

What data is collected, cookies, Google AdSense and DoubleClick cookie disclosure, third-party vendors, GDPR/CCPA rights, children's privacy, and how to opt out of personalised ads.

## Draft notes

Must explicitly mention that Google uses cookies to serve ads based on prior visits, link to Google's Ads Settings, and explain that files are processed locally and never uploaded. Include an effective date and a change-notice clause.

## Minimum content depth

300+ words of specific, human-written text. Name the operator, explain the process, and include a working contact route.
