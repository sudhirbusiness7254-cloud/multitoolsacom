# About MultiTools

**Live URL:** https://multitoolsa.com/about

## What reviewers look for

Who runs the site, why it exists, what makes the tools different, and a real person or team behind it.

## Draft notes

Write 400–600 words in first person. Name the operator, the city, and the reason the site was started (for example, being frustrated by upload-based compressors that kept files). Link to contact and to the tool hub.

## Minimum content depth

300+ words of specific, human-written text. Name the operator, explain the process, and include a working contact route.
