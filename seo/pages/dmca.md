# DMCA / Copyright

**Live URL:** https://multitoolsa.com/dmca

## What reviewers look for

A designated agent, the required notice elements, counter-notice process and repeat-infringer policy.

## Draft notes

Include a real contact channel. A DMCA page with no working email is a common AdSense review flag.

## Minimum content depth

300+ words of specific, human-written text. Name the operator, explain the process, and include a working contact route.
