## What reviewers look for

Acceptable use, no warranty, limitation of liability, intellectual property of uploaded content staying with the user, governing law, and termination.

## Draft notes

State clearly that users must own or have permission for the files they process, and that the service is provided as-is without guarantees of file recovery.

## Minimum content depth

300+ words of specific, human-written text. Name the operator, explain the process, and include a working contact route.
